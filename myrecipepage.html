Chocolate Cake Recipe

Chocolate cake is one of my favorite desserts. It is soft, delicious and easy to prepare at home. This recipe is simple and can be followed by beginners.


Chocolate Cake

About This Recipe

This chocolate cake has a rich chocolate flavor and a soft texture. It is perfect for birthdays, family gatherings and special occasions. 

    Recipe Information
    -------------------------
    Preparation Time : 15 minutes
    Cooking Time     : 35 minutes
    Servings         : 6 - 8 people
    Difficulty       : Easy

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Optional Toppings

  • Chocolate Toppings
    • Chocolate Chips
    • Chocolate Sauce
    • Chocolate Shavings
  • Fruit Toppings
    • Strawberries
    • Bananas
    • Cherries

How to Prepare the Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Take a large bowl and add flour, cocoa powder and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, mix eggs and sugar.
  4. Add milk, oil and vanilla essence.
  5. Mix the wet and dry ingredients together.
  6. Pour the mixture into a greased cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes.
  8. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
